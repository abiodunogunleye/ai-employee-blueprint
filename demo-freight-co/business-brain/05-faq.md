# FAQ

> DEMO ONLY. Invented for a demonstration build. Replace with the
> real owner's questions and answers before use. Answers must
> never contradict 01-business.md or 04-approvals.md.

How to use this file: each answer is how the owner would say it.
"Handling" says what the AI may do. If a question is not in this
file, the AI does not guess. It flags the email "Needs owner".

## 1. How much will it cost to ship my goods?
Answer: Every shipment is quoted individually, so I can't give a
price from a general enquiry. Send me the collection and delivery addresses, the weight and dimensions, what the goods are, and the date it needs to arrive by. Any
quote is valid for 7 days.
Handling: the AI may draft this reply. Any actual price is the
owner's decision.

## 2. How long will it take?
Answer: For road freight, every quote includes an expected
delivery date, and I'll tell you straight away if it changes.
For sea freight I can't promise transit times, because they
depend on carrier schedules and port conditions. I'll update you
as the shipment moves. Air freight is quoted per shipment.
Handling: the AI may draft this reply. Never state a sea transit
time.

## 3. What do you need from me to quote?
Answer:
- Collection and delivery addresses, with postcodes
- Weight and dimensions of each pallet or package
- What the goods are, and whether anything is fragile
- The date it needs to arrive by
Handling: the AI may draft this reply.

## 4. Can you handle customs clearance?
Answer: Yes, for imports and exports. The fee is £35 per
declaration. I'll need the commercial invoice, the packing list,
the commodity code for each product, and your EORI number. If
you're not sure of a commodity code, tell me what the goods are
and we'll work it out together.
Handling: the AI may draft this reply. Customs holds, fines or
seizures are Tier 3, so flag them to the owner.

## 5. Where is my shipment?
Answer: Send me your reference number and I'll tell you its
current status.
Handling: the AI may draft a status update only from information
actually held in our systems. If there is no current status, say
we're checking and will come back by the time set in
01-business.md under "When we're open". Never guess.

## 6. What are your payment terms?
Answer: Approved accounts pay within 30 days. New customers pay
upfront.
Handling: the AI may state these standard terms. Any request for
different terms goes to the owner.

## 7. What can't you ship?
Answer: We don't handle dangerous goods, live animals, or
anything that needs a licence we don't hold. If you're not sure
about your goods, ask me before booking.
Handling: the AI may draft this reply. Anything unclear is
flagged "Needs owner".

## 8. Can you store my goods?
Answer: Yes, we offer short-term warehousing before onward
delivery. Storage is quoted along with your shipment.
Handling: the AI may draft this reply. Never state a storage
price.

## 9. What if my goods are damaged or lost?
Answer: I'm sorry. Please send me your reference number and
photos if you have them, and I'll look into it personally.
Handling: owner only. Flag "Needs owner" immediately. The AI does
not draft a reply unless the owner asks.

## 10. Can I see, correct or delete the information you hold about me?
Answer: Tell me what you'd like, and I'll deal with it
personally.
Handling: owner only, and urgent because there is a legal
deadline. Flag "Needs owner" immediately.
