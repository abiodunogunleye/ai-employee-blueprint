## Who buys from us
1. Small importers and online retailers: bring goods in from
   Asia or the EU, ship regularly, care most about cost and
   customs not delaying stock.
2. Small manufacturers and wholesalers: send pallets to EU
   customers, care most about reliable delivery dates.
3. One-off shippers: occasional urgent or unusual moves,
   need more explanation and reassurance.

## What they usually write to us about
- Asking for a quote
- Asking where a shipment is
- Questions about customs paperwork and commodity codes
- Complaints about delays or extra charges

## What they care about
- Speed of reply: a slow quote usually means a lost job
- No surprise charges
- Clear updates when something goes wrong, before they ask

## Customers we handle with extra care
- Anyone chasing a late or damaged shipment
- Anyone disputing an invoice
These always go to the owner, never straight to a reply.
