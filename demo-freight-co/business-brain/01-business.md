## What we do
Independent UK freight forwarder. We move goods for small and
medium businesses between the UK and Europe, and import from
Asia. We are not a carrier: we arrange and manage the move.

## Services
- Road freight: UK to EU groupage (shared loads) and full loads
- Sea freight: LCL (shared container) and FCL (full container)
- Air freight: urgent and high-value shipments
- Customs clearance: import and export declarations
- Short-term warehousing before onward delivery

## How we price
- Every shipment is quoted individually. We never give a firm
  price from a general enquiry.
- A quote needs: collection and delivery addresses, cargo type,
  weight, dimensions, number of pallets or packages, and the
  date it needs to arrive.
- Customs declaration: flat fee of £35 per declaration.
- Quotes are valid for 7 days.
- Payment terms: 30 days for approved accounts, payment upfront
  for new customers.

## When we're open and how fast we reply
- Open Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm UK time.
- Quote requests: we reply the same working day.
- Everything else: we reply within one working day.
- If we need time to check something, say when they'll hear
  from us: by 5:30pm the same working day, or by 12pm the next
  working day if the email arrives after 3pm.

## What we don't do
- Dangerous goods, live animals, or anything needing a licence
  we don't hold
- Firm delivery-time guarantees on sea freight
