# Voice

## How we sound
- Friendly, direct, plain English. UK spelling. Short sentences.
- We sound like a person who knows freight, not a call centre.
- No jargon unless the customer used it first. Explain a term once
  in plain words (for example: "groupage means your pallets share
  a lorry with other loads").
- Answer first, detail after.
- Sign off with first name only: "Best wishes, Sam".

## Do
- Put the answer or the next step in the first line.
- Give real dates, times and numbers, never "soon" or "shortly".
- Ask for what we need as a short list.
- Own problems plainly: what happened, what we're doing about it,
  and when they'll hear from us next.

## Don't
- Don't give a firm price from a general enquiry, or promise
  sea transit times.
- Don't use filler such as "I hope this email finds you well" or
  "per my last email".
- Don't blame the carrier, the port or the customer.
- Don't use more than one exclamation mark in an email.

## Sample emails in the owner's voice

### 1. Replying to a quote request
Subject: Re: Quote for 6 pallets, Manchester to Rotterdam

Hi Priya,

Thanks for getting in touch. Happy to quote this.

To give you an accurate price I need:
- Collection and delivery addresses, with postcodes
- Weight and dimensions of each pallet
- What's on the pallets, and whether anything is fragile
- The date it needs to arrive by

Send those over and I'll come back to you the same working day.
Any quote I send is valid for 7 days.

Best wishes,
Sam

### 2. Shipment update
Subject: Update on your Felixstowe container

Hi Priya,

Quick update: your container cleared customs this morning and is
booked for delivery to your warehouse on Thursday between 8am and
12pm.

Nothing needed from you. If anything changes, I'll let you know
straight away.

Best wishes,
Sam

### 3. A delay
Subject: Your shipment: a day's delay and what we're doing

Hi Priya,

I'm sorry, but your pallets will arrive a day later than I told
you. Yesterday's ferry crossing was cancelled, and the next
available sailing is tonight.

New delivery is Friday morning. I'll confirm as soon as they're
on the boat, and I'll check in again on Thursday at 4pm either way.

If Friday doesn't work for you, tell me and we'll look at options.
I'd rather you heard this from me now than found out on the day.

Best wishes,
Sam
